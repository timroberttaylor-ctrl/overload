OVRLD

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 (Apple Health and lock-screen timer added)

In short: OVRLD does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to export it.

This policy explains how the OVRLD strength-training app for iPhone and Android (“the app”, “we”) handles information. The app is developed by Tim Taylor in Australia.

Information the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Workouts you log: exercises, sets, weights, reps, effort (RPE), rest times and notes.
  • Programs you start or build, and your weekly check-ins (for example fatigue, soreness and joint pain ratings).
  • Body-weight entries, if you enter them.
  • Custom exercises, video links you add, and your app settings.

We never receive this information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is protected by your device's own security and encryption.

Information we collect

None. The app makes no network requests of its own, has no sign-in, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software. We do not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local alert on your phone when a rest period ends. These are created on the device; no notification service or push server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in your phone's Settings.

Apple Health (optional)

On iPhone you can turn on Settings → Apple Health → Save finished workouts. When it is on, each workout you finish is saved to Apple Health on your phone as a strength-training workout with its start and end time. OVRLD only writes workouts: it does not read any of your Health data. Data shared with Apple Health is never used for advertising, never sold, never sent to us or to any third party, and OVRLD does not store it in iCloud. You can turn this off in OVRLD at any time, and manage or remove OVRLD's access in the Health app (Profile → Apps → OVRLD). Workouts already saved stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.

Lock-screen rest timer

On iPhone, the rest timer can appear on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity. It shows only the countdown and the name of your next exercise, is created on your phone, and ends when your rest does. You can turn Live Activities off for OVRLD in the iPhone Settings app.

Exports and backups

You can export a backup (JSON) or your workout log (CSV) from Settings. An export happens only when you tap it, and the file goes wherever you choose through your phone's share sheet (for example Files, email or a cloud drive). Once exported, that copy is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy terms. Export files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust. Temporary export files are deleted from the app's cache automatically.

Links

If you add or open an exercise video link, it opens in your browser or the relevant app (for example YouTube), which has its own privacy policy. The app only opens web (https) links.

Health information

Training data such as body weight, effort ratings and joint-pain check-ins can be health-related. It is used only inside the app to suggest your training, and never leaves your device unless you export it. If you turn on Apple Health, the app writes your finished workouts there (see above); it never reads from Apple Health or Google Health Connect.

Deleting your data

Because all data is on your device, you are in full control. Deleting the app removes all of its data. You can also remove individual workouts, exercises and programs inside the app. On Android, the app's data is excluded from automatic cloud backup.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. This is consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles, the GDPR and similar laws.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that handles data differently (for example, connecting a wearable such as an Oura ring), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change. Any such feature will be optional and will ask for your permission first.
